# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project harassment-free for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality,
appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes demonstrating empathy, respecting differing opinions,
accepting responsibility, focusing on community benefit, and giving constructive
feedback.

Unacceptable behavior includes sexualized language or attention, trolling or
insults, harassment, publishing another person's private information, and other
conduct reasonably considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Project maintainers will clarify and enforce these standards. They may remove,
edit, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors
for behavior they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

## Scope

This Code applies in project spaces and when an individual officially represents
the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior through GitHub's
private reporting tools. Maintainers will review reports promptly and fairly,
respect reporter privacy, and avoid conflicts of interest.

Enforcement follows the Contributor Covenant Community Impact Guidelines: correction,
warning, temporary ban, or permanent ban according to impact and recurrence.
